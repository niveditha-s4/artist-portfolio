# Artist Portfolio — Niveditha

## Project Overview

This is a personal portfolio website for **Niveditha**, a passionate visual artist specializing in **wall art and murals**. Her goal is to establish herself as a professional **wall muralist**, and this portfolio is her primary showcase for attracting clients, collaborators, and opportunities.

## About the Artist

- **Name:** Niveditha
- **Specialization:** Wall art & large-scale murals
- **Style:** Expressive, hand-crafted wall paintings
- **Goal:** Become a recognized professional wall muralist

## Project Purpose

The portfolio should:
- Showcase Niveditha's wall art and mural work prominently
- Communicate her artistic style, passion, and vision
- Make it easy for potential clients to contact her for commissions
- Feel warm, creative, and personal — reflecting her as an artist

## Design Guidelines

- Keep the aesthetic artistic and expressive — avoid overly corporate or minimal styles
- Color palette should complement and not compete with the artwork
- Typography should feel hand-crafted or painterly where appropriate
- The artwork/portfolio gallery is the hero of the page — keep UI secondary
- Mobile-responsive is essential; murals are often discovered on phones

## Content Priorities (in order)

1. Hero section — strong visual impact with her best work
2. Portfolio/gallery — wall art and mural pieces front and center
3. About — her story, passion, and journey toward becoming a muralist
4. Services/commissions — what she offers and how to hire her
5. Contact — simple, accessible call to action

## Tone & Voice

- Warm, passionate, and authentic
- Artist-first — let the work speak
- Avoid overly formal or sales-heavy language
