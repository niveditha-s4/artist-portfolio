MEET THE ARTIST

The Passion Behind the Brush

Hi, I am S. Niveditha S. Drawing on walls isn't just what I do; it is how I communicate with space.

Since childhood, standard-size paper felt like a confinement. I wanted my imagination to span across structures. That's how I found my true calling in Wall Mural Art. Walls represent raw canvases that interact with light, depth, furniture, and human movement.

I specialize in crafting bespoke murals that turn everyday spaces—from living rooms and study corners to washbasins and commercial cafe walls—into tailored art galleries. My designs are custom-sketched to fit the unique geometry, lighting, and personality of your space.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know Before Commissioning a Mural

Straight answers to the questions clients ask most before booking their custom wall mural.

Murals are priced by complexity and square footage. Minimalist line art and botanical murals start at ₹80/sq.ft. Detailed floral murals are priced at ₹150/sq.ft. Narrative character murals (Harry Potter, fantasy, custom stories) cost ₹350/sq.ft. Use the interactive Mural Configurator above to calculate your exact estimate instantly.

Every mural is 100% freehand and handcrafted — no projectors, no stencils, no decals. Each design is sketched and painted directly on your wall, making every piece a completely unique original artwork tailored to your space.

Any flat wall surface is a canvas. S. Niveditha S paints murals in living rooms, bedrooms, nurseries, study nooks, washbasin accent walls, dining rooms, commercial offices, cafes, gyms, hotel lobbies, and retail stores. Both residential and commercial projects are welcome across India.

Timeline varies by size and complexity. A small minimalist or accent wall typically takes 1–2 days. Detailed or large narrative murals (full-room themes) can take 3–7 days. The process always begins with a free design consultation and sketch session before painting starts.

The process begins with a discovery consultation covering your vision, wall dimensions, lighting, and theme. S. Niveditha S then prepares a custom concept sketch for your approval. Once confirmed, she paints the mural on-site from base coat to final detailing — and cleans up completely after completion.
